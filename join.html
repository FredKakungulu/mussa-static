Join & support MUSSA

Become a fully fledged member of the Makerere University School of Statistics Association, and help sustain the programmes that serve students of the School of Statistics and Planning.

How to subscribe

There is no online payment form on this site. To subscribe, reach out to your class representative. Class reps are part of the association cabinet and can guide you through the current subscription process and any requirements.

Talk to your class rep

Ask how to become a member, what support looks like this semester, and how dues or registration (if any) are handled. They are your first and best point of contact for joining MUSSA.

Ways to support the association

Membership is the foundation — staying involved keeps MUSSA active for everyone.

Subscribe as a member

Confirm your membership through your class representative so you are counted as a fully fledged member.

Show up for activities

Attend academic, social, and welfare events. Participation is one of the strongest forms of support.

Follow & share updates

Follow MUSSA on social media so notices, programmes, and election announcements reach your cohort.

Follow MUSSA

Stay close to association news and activities. Links are placeholders for now — real profiles will be added soon.

Know the election path too

Membership and voting are related but not the same: elections use the Electoral Commission voter register. Learn the booth process so you are ready when polls open.
